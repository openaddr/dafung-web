## 新图

使用内置 ImageGen，以[屯粮居奇](https://github.com/openaddr/dafung-web/blob/952817c1049e010b269b5876eca864c5ca6e0bca/docs/design/ui-reference/prototype-1409-1411/encounters/art/grain.webp)和[华容道](https://github.com/openaddr/dafung-web/blob/952817c1049e010b269b5876eca864c5ca6e0bca/docs/design/ui-reference/prototype-1409-1411/encounters/art/huarong.webp)作风格参考。各图集为 1024×1536 的两列四行图，按从左到右、从上到下取格。每格四边内裁 2px，缩至 480×359，编码为 WebP（ImageMagick，质量 77）。各机遇的主体单独构图。

生成记录均位于 `01a1f81f-4435-7963-8852-95fb403c76e4/`。共用提示词之后附上各批次的八个场景提示词，序号对应图集格位。

```text
Create one original production illustration atlas for a late Eastern Han / Three Kingdoms Chinese historical game. Strict full-bleed contact sheet, portrait canvas ratio 2:3, exactly TWO columns and FOUR rows of equally sized landscape 4:3 panels, no borders, frames, gutters, letters, logos, titles, graphic overlays or symbols. Perfect straight grid breaks: x=50%, y=25%, 50%, 75%. Eight fully separate images, no objects crossing between them. Compose each for instant narrative recognition at 100 px, clear large dominant figures/objects, centered action, consistent depth and legibility. Restrained mineral pigment and aged silk, dense hand-painted ink contours, muted earthy browns and dark blue-grey, realistic late Han clothes, architecture and tools, richly textured but not photographic, lightly weathered, atmospheric Chinese historical book illustration. Match the style of the reference artwork; reference is style only, content follows individual cells. Each numbered line is its own cell in left-to-right top-to-bottom order.
```
